What the labels mean.

No label

Validated with Mona Radio on real hardware.

Preview

Available in Mona Radio today. Validation across more hardware, firmware and connections continues, and some features may differ from model to model.

Receive Only

A receiver. Mona Radio follows its frequency, spectrum and audio, and never offers transmit for it.

Preview · Receive Only

A receiver that is also still in Preview: available today, with validation continuing, and never offered transmit.
