Mona Relay Preview

One connection. Every screen.

Your Mac holds the connection to your radio. Mona Relay shares it — live state, spectrum, waterfall and receive audio — with the iPhone, iPad and Mac you choose.

  • Paired, not just found. Each device is approved on your Mac with a verification code.
  • Encrypted on your network. Mona Relay stays on your local network, with no Mona Apps server in between.
  • One radio session. The radio sees a single connection, however many screens are watching.
  • Listen-only sharing. Shared devices receive; control stays with the Mac connected to the radio.

Mona Relay is in Preview. Availability and features may change as validation continues.
